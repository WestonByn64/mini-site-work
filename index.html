You Should Play Deadlock!

This website is about Deadlock! It's a MOBA in development by Valve in early access. It recently had its newest major update, "City Never Sleeps," a couple of weeks ago, adding 6 new characters to the already large roster. In fact, the vote for which of the last 2 characters to be added first ends tomorrow. I'd highly recommend giving it a shot if you haven't already!
